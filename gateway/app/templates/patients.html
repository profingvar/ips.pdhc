Generate Mock Patients

Creates patients linked to the chosen organisation. Names sampled uniquely from a combinatorial Swedish-name pool (up to 150 per batch). Default mode also generates full IPS clinical data per patient (conditions, medications, allergies, immunizations, observations, procedures, diagnostic reports, IPS card + snapshot). Tick "Skip clinical data" to create just the Patient + clinic assignment — appropriate when sim.pdhc or another upstream generator will provide the clinical data.

{# #811: an AGE range, not birth years — an operator thinks "40 to 75", and converting to birth years by hand is both annoying and the kind of arithmetic that drifts a year every January. Blank means the default span (16–86), which is what the old hardcoded 1940–2010 birth years amounted to, so leaving these empty behaves as before. #}

Generation batches

Each batch is one click of Generate Mock Patients. euIPS counts how many carry all three required sections (allergies, problems, medications) as content or as an explicit “none known”. Inspect filters the patient list below to that batch — its patients are otherwise mixed into the list like any other.

Purge is a permanent delete, and is not Archive. It removes the patients, their clinical resources, their clinic assignments, their IPS cards and their snapshots. There is no undo and no backup, and anything already pushed downstream to cdr_6 stays there pointing at patients that no longer exist. To tidy the list without losing data, use Archive on the individual rows instead.
