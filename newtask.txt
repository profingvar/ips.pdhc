NEXT PHASE: THIS SERVICE BECOMES eps.pdhc
------------------------------------------
Operator decision, 2026-11-04, following the EPS work (#876 epic, #905).

Measured scope: 845 occurrences across 16 top-level directories. Only 189 of
them are in THIS repo - the cost is in the eight sibling services that carry
IPS_BASE_URL and IPS_API_KEY in their own .env files and clients, plus the
website.

READ progress.md's "NEXT PHASE" section before touching anything. It carries
the measurement, the six decisions that come before any rename, and the
precedent: the gateway -> planp rename of March 2026 changed the Docker
volume namespace and a cp in the wrong direction destroyed a production
database irrecoverably. Plan's database is still called `pdhc_gateway` eight
months later.

The short version of the decisions:
  1. Does the data move at all? (ips_pgdata, ips-db-1, ips_db, ips_user.)
     Keeping them is ugly and safe. Plan proves ugly-and-safe survives years.
  2. New eps.pdhc.se vhost + TLS cert, with ips.pdhc.se kept answering until
     every caller has moved. Both live at once, or callers break one by one.
  3. sso.pdhc needs an _EPS client pair registered FIRST, by the operator -
     sso is outside Claude's envelope (CLAUDE.md §15).
  4. Eight .env files accept BOTH names, repoint one service per run, each
     verified by its own deploy/smoke_siblings.py before and after, then
     remove the old name.
  5. CLAUDE.md §3 and the website's service list.
  6. Leave #-numbers in comments alone. They record when a decision was
     taken (CLAUDE.md §2).

Do NOT start with a sed. The #816 split showed a blind replace breaks correct
references that merely share a string, and CLAUDE.md's KNOWN_SERVICES table
was wrong twice because a hand survey globbed a filename instead of searching
for the symbol.
